光伏并网逆变器建模与功率控制仿真研究 Grid-Connected PV Inverter Modelling and Power Control Simulation

本项目围绕接入 380V 交流母线的三相分布式光伏并网逆变系统展开，使用 PSCAD/EMTDC 建立逆变器主电路、坐标变换、功率外环、电流内环和 SPWM 调制模型。项目基于 abc、αβ 和 dq 坐标系下的逆变器数学模型，设计电网电压定向的 PQ 控制与前馈解耦电流控制，并通过仿真观察有功功率、无功功率和 dq 轴电流的动态响应。 This academic engineering simulation project focuses on a three-phase grid-connected PV inverter connected to a 380V AC bus. PSCAD/EMTDC was used to build the inverter main circuit, coordinate transformation, power outer loop, current inner loop and SPWM modulation model. The work analyses active power, reactive power and dq-axis current responses under selected operating conditions.

问题背景Problem Context

光伏发电系统需要通过并网逆变器把直流侧能量转换成满足电网电压、频率和电能质量要求的交流电。对于分布式光伏和光储系统，逆变器不仅承担能量转换功能，还需要控制有功功率输出、处理无功功率需求，并保持并网电流与电网条件协调。 PV systems require grid-connected inverters to convert DC-side energy into AC power that meets grid voltage, frequency and power-quality requirements. In distributed PV and PV-storage systems, the inverter also needs to regulate active power, handle reactive-power requirements and coordinate grid current with network conditions.

技术流程Technical Workflow

阶段一Stage 1 建立系统结构与数学模型。输入三相 PWM 电压型逆变器结构、并网电压条件、滤波电感、电网电压和直流侧输入假设；基于 KVL/KCL 建立 abc 坐标系模型，并通过 Clark 和 Park 变换转到 αβ 与 dq 坐标系。 System setup and mathematical modelling. The abc-frame model was developed from the three-phase PWM voltage-source inverter structure, grid conditions and filter assumptions, then transformed into αβ and dq frames using Clark and Park transformations.
阶段二Stage 2 设计功率控制策略。基于 dq 坐标系模型和瞬时有功、无功功率关系，采用电网电压定向控制和 PQ 控制，将功率控制转化为 q 轴和 d 轴电流控制。 Power-control strategy design. Based on the dq-frame model and instantaneous active/reactive power relationships, grid-voltage-oriented PQ control was used to map power control into q-axis and d-axis current control.
阶段三Stage 3 搭建 PSCAD 仿真模型。将主电路、坐标变换模块、功率外环、电流内环和 SPWM 调制模块组合为完整仿真系统，并配置 380V、50Hz、0.5MVA、1mH、25μF、780V 和 0.3MW 等工况参数。 PSCAD simulation model construction. The main circuit, coordinate transformation, power outer loop, current inner loop and SPWM modulation blocks were combined into one simulation system with 380V, 50Hz, 0.5MVA, 1mH, 25μF, 780V and 0.3MW settings.
阶段四Stage 4 分析仿真结果。比较参考值与实际输出曲线，观察 0.3MW 有功功率、0MVar 无功功率、dq 轴电流以及 3s 时从 0.3MW 到 0.35MW 阶跃变化下的动态响应。 Simulation result interpretation. Reference and actual output curves were compared for 0.3MW active power, 0MVar reactive power, dq-axis current tracking and a 3s step change from 0.3MW to 0.35MW.

常见问题FAQ

这个项目研究的是什么？What was the purpose of this project?
这个项目研究三相光伏并网逆变器如何通过建模和控制策略实现稳定并网。项目从逆变器主电路和数学模型出发，分析 abc、αβ 和 dq 坐标系之间的关系，并使用 PSCAD/EMTDC 搭建仿真模型。重点观察有功功率、无功功率和 dq 轴电流在控制系统中的跟踪表现。 The project studies how a three-phase PV grid-connected inverter can be modelled and controlled for grid connection. It starts from the inverter circuit and mathematical model, then analyses abc, αβ and dq frame relationships before building the PSCAD/EMTDC simulation model.
为什么并网逆变器控制重要？Why is inverter control important?
光伏系统输出的是直流电，而电网需要稳定的交流电。并网逆变器负责电能转换，也需要让输出电压、频率、电流和功率满足并网条件。控制策略会影响有功功率注入、无功功率调节和电流质量，因此它是分布式光伏接入和新能源电力电子系统中的关键环节。 PV systems produce DC power while the grid requires stable AC power. A grid-connected inverter performs conversion and must keep voltage, frequency, current and power compatible with grid requirements. Control strategy affects active-power injection, reactive-power regulation and current quality.
为什么要把 abc 坐标系转换到 dq 坐标系？Why transform abc variables into the dq frame?
三相逆变器在 abc 坐标系下的电压和电流是交流量，直接控制会受到相位变化和变量耦合影响。通过 Clark 变换和 Park 变换，可以把三相变量转换到同步旋转 dq 坐标系，使稳态量更适合 PI 控制，并便于将功率控制问题转化为电流分量控制问题。 In the abc frame, inverter voltages and currents are sinusoidal and coupled. Clark and Park transformations convert three-phase variables into the synchronous dq frame, where steady-state quantities are easier to regulate with PI controllers and power control can be mapped to current components.
仿真结果怎样体现功率跟踪？How do the results show power tracking?
在 0.3MW 有功功率参考下，仿真曲线显示实际有功功率经过暂态调整后趋近参考值。无功功率参考为 0MVar，输出曲线在调整后也趋近目标。进一步的阶跃工况中，有功功率参考值在 3s 从 0.3MW 变化到 0.35MW，实际输出经过短暂调整后趋近新的参考值。 With a 0.3MW active-power reference, the simulated output approaches the reference after transient adjustment. The reactive-power curve also approaches the 0MVar target. In the step case, the active-power reference changes from 0.3MW to 0.35MW at 3s and the output approaches the new target.
如果进一步扩展，会优先改进什么？What would be improved in a more advanced version?
进一步工作可以从更复杂的滤波器、更真实的电网条件和更完整的光伏侧模型入手。例如引入 LCL 滤波器、考虑三相电网电压不对称工况，或者把光伏阵列和储能单元从恒压源简化模型扩展为更完整的动态模型。 Future work could extend the filter model, grid-condition assumptions and PV-side dynamics. Examples include LCL filtering, unbalanced three-phase grid voltage scenarios, and replacing the simplified DC source with more complete PV-array and storage dynamics.
