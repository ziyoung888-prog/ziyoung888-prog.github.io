电力系统潮流、故障、电压控制与安全经济调度分析 Load Flow, Fault, Voltage Control and Secure Economic Dispatch Analysis

该学术工程项目整合电力系统稳态潮流、导纳矩阵建模、短路故障水平、时序负荷、电压稳定、变压器有载调压、经济调度和分布式电源并网影响分析。项目使用 PowerWorld 与 MATLAB 将网络参数、计算结果和运行约束组织成可复核的工程仿真流程。 This portfolio consolidates four coursework reports into one engineering case study covering steady-state load flow, admittance-matrix modelling, short-circuit fault level, time-varying load, voltage stability, OLTC voltage control, economic dispatch and distributed-generation impacts. The work translates textbook equations into auditable PowerWorld and MATLAB analysis workflows.

项目概览Project Overview

问题背景Problem Context
课程要求在多个给定电网算例中分析潮流、线路约束、故障水平、电压稳定和运行经济性，任务接近电网规划与运行工程师日常面对的仿真分析问题。 The course required analysis of load flow, line constraints, fault levels, voltage stability and operating economics across several network cases, matching practical simulation tasks in grid planning and operations.
分析任务Task
我需要把线路阻抗、负荷、发电机、变压器和控制约束转化为可计算模型，并解释每一次仿真变化背后的电气机理，而不是只报告软件截图。 I needed to convert line impedance, load, generators, transformers and operating constraints into computable models, then explain the electrical mechanism behind each simulation result rather than only present screenshots.
技术执行Action
使用手算、MATLAB 和 PowerWorld 交叉验证母线电压、相角、线路潮流、无功支撑、故障电流、过载路径和调度成本，并在高负荷、故障、无功需求上升和发电重分配场景中判断系统风险。 I cross-checked bus voltage, angle, line flow, reactive support, fault current, overload paths and dispatch cost using manual calculations, MATLAB and PowerWorld, then assessed system risk under high-load, fault, reactive-demand and redispatch scenarios.
项目输出Result
最终输出覆盖从三母线系统基尔霍夫平衡校验，到较大网络的时序电压、OLTC 调压、经济调度与安全约束冲突分析。 The final output spans Kirchhoff balance checks in a three-bus case, time-series voltage assessment, OLTC control, economic dispatch and security-constraint analysis in larger networks.

技术贡献Technical Contribution

  • 建立三母线和多母线网络模型，识别 Slack、PV、PQ 节点，并把线路 R/X 参数换算为标幺值。Built three-bus and multi-bus network models, identified Slack, PV and PQ buses, and converted line R/X data into per-unit values.
  • 用 MATLAB 求解导纳矩阵、节点电压、线路电流和复功率，并与 PowerWorld 输出进行一致性检查。Used MATLAB to solve admittance matrices, bus voltages, line currents and complex power flows, then checked consistency against PowerWorld output.
  • 对短路故障、负荷切除、发电机断开和高无功负荷场景进行分析，解释过载、电压跌落和电压崩溃风险。Analysed short-circuit faults, load disconnection, generator outage and high-reactive-load cases, explaining overload, voltage sag and voltage-collapse risk.
  • 在经济调度结果导致线路超载时，手动重分配发电出力，使线路负载回到 100% MVA 限值以内，并说明成本与安全性的取舍。When economic dispatch created overloads, I manually redistributed generator outputs to bring line loading back within the 100% MVA limit and explained the cost-security trade-off.

综合技术流程Integrated Technical Workflow

阶段一Stage 1 从三母线系统入手，计算线路标幺阻抗、节点类型、功率平衡和线路潮流；用 PowerWorld 对比手算结果，验证平衡母线对损耗和平衡的作用。Started with a three-bus system: per-unit line impedance, bus type classification, power balance and line-flow calculation; compared manual results with PowerWorld and verified the role of the slack bus in loss balancing.
阶段二Stage 2 把网络表示为导纳矩阵和节点方程，用 MATLAB 求解电压、电流和功率流；随后在 PowerWorld 中做故障水平、负荷切除、发电机断开和电压稳定分析。Represented the network using admittance matrices and nodal equations, solved voltages, currents and power flows in MATLAB, then used PowerWorld for fault-level, load-disconnection, generator-outage and voltage-stability studies.
阶段三Stage 3 处理 24 小时时序负荷，把标幺负荷转换为 MW/MVAr，比较平均与最大负荷工况，分析低压母线、T3 变压器和 L67 线路的运行压力，并用 OLTC 改善电压。Handled 24-hour time-varying loads, converted per-unit load into MW/MVAr, compared average and maximum loading, analysed stress on low-voltage buses, transformer T3 and line L67, and used OLTC action to improve voltage.
阶段四Stage 4 使用 MATLAB 拉格朗日法求经济调度，再在 PowerWorld 中研究高负荷网络约束。通过降低北部发电、提高南部发电，把多条超载线路恢复到安全范围，并讨论 DG 并网对故障水平和保护的影响。Used a MATLAB Lagrange method for economic dispatch, then studied network constraints under high load in PowerWorld. By reducing northern generation and increasing southern generation, overloaded lines were restored to safe limits, followed by a discussion of DG impacts on fault level and protection.

工具与方法Tools and Methods

  • PowerWorld：搭建网络拓扑、输入发电机/负荷/线路/变压器参数，执行潮流、故障、差分模式和调度场景分析。PowerWorld: built network topology, entered generator/load/line/transformer data, and ran load-flow, fault, difference-mode and dispatch scenario studies.
  • MATLAB：构建 Ybus 矩阵、求解节点方程、计算线路电流和复功率，并实现拉格朗日经济调度计算。MATLAB: built Ybus matrices, solved nodal equations, calculated line current and complex power, and implemented Lagrange economic dispatch calculations.
  • 工程判断：把电压跌落、线路过载、无功不足和网络拥塞解释为物理问题，而不是只依赖软件输出。Engineering judgement: interpreted voltage drop, line overload, reactive-power shortage and network congestion as physical problems, not just software outputs.

工程应用能力Engineering Application Areas

  • 电力系统仿真：能把题目参数整理成可运行模型，并检查模型是否满足功率平衡和运行约束。Power-system simulation: converting specifications into executable models and checking power balance and operating constraints.
  • 运行分析：能识别线路、变压器、母线电压和无功支撑的薄弱环节。Operational analysis: identifying weak points in lines, transformers, bus voltage and reactive support.
  • 技术沟通：能把公式、仿真截图和结论组织成英文工程报告。Technical communication: organising equations, simulation evidence and conclusions into English engineering reports.

代表性输出Selected Outputs

潮流校验Load-Flow Verification 在三母线算例中，将手算线路潮流与 PowerWorld 仿真结果进行对比，检查节点功率输入输出是否满足基尔霍夫平衡。In the three-bus case, manual line-flow calculations were compared with PowerWorld results to check whether bus-level power input and output satisfied Kirchhoff balance.
电压稳定Voltage Stability 随着无功负荷升高，远端母线电压从接近额定值逐步跌落，并在高无功区间表现出电压崩溃风险，说明本地无功补偿和电压控制的重要性。As reactive load increased, remote-bus voltage fell from near-rated levels toward a voltage-collapse risk region, highlighting the importance of local reactive compensation and voltage control.
时序负荷Time-Series Loading 时序负荷分析识别出高负荷时段集中在上午到下午，T3 变压器和 33 kV 线路 L67 接近额定容量，低压端母线更容易出现电压偏低。The time-series loading study identified that high loading concentrated from morning to afternoon, transformer T3 and 33 kV line L67 approached rated capacity, and downstream low-voltage buses were more exposed to undervoltage.
安全调度Secure Dispatch 经济调度场景导致母线 1-3、母线 1-2、母线 2-5 等线路严重超载；通过降低 G1/G2、提高 G6 输出，最终把所有线路负载降至 100% MVA 限值以内，小时成本从 26,465 英镑/小时增至 28,817 英镑/小时。The economic-dispatch scenario overloaded lines such as Bus 1-3, Bus 1-2 and Bus 2-5. By reducing G1/G2 and increasing G6 output, all line loading was brought within the 100% MVA limit, while hourly cost increased from 26,465 GBP/h to 28,817 GBP/h.
新能源并网DG Integration 报告讨论了分布式光伏、风电等 DG 接入后对双向潮流、故障电流、保护配合和传统继电保护策略的影响。The reports discussed how distributed PV, wind and other DG sources affect bidirectional power flow, fault current, protection coordination and conventional relay strategies.

项目模块Project Modules

阶段一 / 潮流分析Stage 1 / Load Flow
三母线潮流计算与 PowerWorld 校验Three-Bus Load Flow and PowerWorld Verification

基于线路 R/X、负荷和发电机参数建立三母线输电网络，结合手算和 PowerWorld 输出检查潮流方向、节点平衡和相角变化。Built a three-bus transmission-network case from line R/X, load and generator data, then compared manual calculations with PowerWorld outputs for line flow, nodal balance and angle changes.

  • 输入：100 MVA、400 kV 基准值，线路长度、R/X 参数、母线 1/2/3 负荷与发电机设置。Inputs: 100 MVA and 400 kV base values, line lengths, R/X parameters, and bus-level load/generation settings.
  • 方法：阻抗标幺化、Slack/PV/PQ 节点识别、线路潮流手算、PowerWorld 结果对比。Method: per-unit impedance conversion, Slack/PV/PQ bus classification, manual line-flow calculation and PowerWorld comparison.
  • 输出：对母线 3 发电增减 50 MW 和母线 2 负荷变化后的相角、潮流和平衡母线出力变化进行解释。Output: interpreted angle, line-flow and slack-bus response after +/-50 MW generation changes at Bus 3 and load changes at Bus 2.
阶段二 / 故障与稳定性Stage 2 / Fault & Stability
导纳矩阵、故障水平与电压稳定分析Admittance Matrix, Fault Level and Voltage Stability

使用 MATLAB 求解 Ybus 节点方程，并在 PowerWorld 中分析故障水平、负荷切除、发电机断开和高无功负荷下的电压响应。Solved Ybus-based nodal equations in MATLAB and used PowerWorld to study fault level, load disconnection, generator outage and voltage response under high reactive loading.

  • 输入：节点导纳矩阵、节点电流、线路参数、PowerWorld 多母线网络数据。Inputs: bus admittance matrix, nodal currents, line parameters and PowerWorld multi-bus network data.
  • 方法：矩阵求逆求节点电压，使用 Ohm 定律计算线路电流，用 S = V I* 计算复功率。Method: matrix inversion for bus voltages, Ohm's law for line currents and S = V I* for complex power.
  • 输出：MATLAB 得到 V1=0.9744∠-17.7638°、V4=0.9527∠-20.1599° 等电压结果；高无功负荷下母线 13 电压跌至 0.6 pu 以下。Output: MATLAB produced voltage results such as V1=0.9744∠-17.7638° and V4=0.9527∠-20.1599°; Bus 13 voltage fell below 0.6 pu under high reactive loading.
阶段三 / 时序运行分析Stage 3 / Time-Series Operation
24 小时时序负荷、电压薄弱点与 OLTC 调压24-Hour Loading, Weak Voltage Points and OLTC Control

将 24 小时负荷曲线转换为 MW/MVAr 输入，比较平均与最大负荷工况，并评估 OLTC 对远端母线电压的改善效果。Converted 24-hour load curves into MW/MVAr inputs, compared average and maximum loading cases, and evaluated OLTC impact on downstream voltage profiles.

  • 输入：Bus 1/2/3/6/8/10 的时序负荷数据、功率因数、T3 变压器和 L67 线路运行结果。Inputs: time-varying load data for Buses 1/2/3/6/8/10, power factors, and operating results for transformer T3 and line L67.
  • 方法：标幺负荷转换、平均/最大负荷工况对比、线路和变压器负载曲线分析、OLTC 调压仿真。Method: per-unit load conversion, average/maximum loading comparison, line and transformer loading analysis, and OLTC voltage-control simulation.
  • 输出：11:00 被识别为最严重工况；9-10 线路负载率达到 110.79%；OLTC 后 Bus 8/9/10 电压改善到约 0.99793/0.97571/0.95339 pu。Output: 11:00 was identified as the worst condition; line 9-10 reached 110.79% loading; after OLTC, Bus 8/9/10 improved to about 0.99793/0.97571/0.95339 pu.
阶段四 / 调度与分布式电源Stage 4 / Dispatch & DG
经济调度、网络约束重调度与 DG 并网影响Economic Dispatch, Network-Constrained Redispatch and DG Impact

对比经济调度与网络约束调度结果，分析线路过载、调度成本、边际成本和分布式电源接入对保护策略的影响。Compared economic dispatch and network-constrained operation, analysing line overloads, dispatch cost, marginal cost and distributed-generation impacts on protection strategy.

  • 输入：两母线经济调度算例、B7FLAT 网络、180% 负荷场景、发电机成本曲线和线路 MVA 限值。Inputs: two-bus economic-dispatch case, B7FLAT network, 180% loading scenario, generator cost curves and line MVA limits.
  • 方法：MATLAB 拉格朗日法、PowerWorld 经济调度/最优潮流设置、高负荷场景潮流检查、发电出力重分配。Method: MATLAB Lagrange calculation, PowerWorld ED/OPF setup, high-load flow checks and generator-output redispatch.
  • 输出：通过降低 G1/G2、提高 G6 输出，所有线路恢复至 100% MVA 限值以内；小时成本从 26,465 英镑/小时增至 28,817 英镑/小时。Output: reducing G1/G2 and increasing G6 restored all lines within the 100% MVA limit; hourly cost increased from 26,465 GBP/h to 28,817 GBP/h.

公开常见问题Public FAQ

这个项目主要分析什么问题？What was the purpose of this project?

项目分析电力系统在不同网络结构、负荷水平、电压约束和调度假设下的运行状态变化。仿真部分使用 PowerWorld，矩阵计算和经济调度计算使用 MATLAB，并把潮流结果、故障水平评估、电压变化和网络约束调度组织成分阶段的分析流程。The project examined how power-system operating conditions change under different network, load, voltage and dispatch assumptions. It used PowerWorld for simulation and MATLAB for matrix-based and economic-dispatch calculations, linking load-flow results, fault-level assessment, voltage behaviour and network-constrained operation into one staged analysis workflow.

主要输出包括哪些内容？What were the main outputs?

主要输出包括基准潮流模型、基于 Ybus 的 MATLAB 电压与功率流计算、PowerWorld 故障与电压稳定分析、24 小时时序负荷分析、OLTC 调压前后对比、经济调度与网络约束重调度结果，以及分布式电源接入对故障水平和保护配合影响的讨论。The outputs include a baseline load-flow model, Ybus-based MATLAB voltage and power-flow calculations, PowerWorld fault and voltage-stability studies, 24-hour loading analysis, OLTC voltage-control comparison, economic dispatch and network-constrained redispatch results, and a short discussion of distributed generation impacts on fault level and protection coordination.

它和工程岗位有什么关系？How is it relevant to engineering roles?

该项目对应电力系统分析、新能源并网、配电网规划、技术支持和工程仿真等工作场景。内容覆盖网络建模、电压变化、线路负载、故障水平评估和调度约束，这些都是并网研究和电力系统运行分析中常见的技术问题。The project is relevant to roles involving power-system analysis, renewable energy integration, distribution network planning, technical support and engineering simulation. It connects network modelling, voltage behaviour, line loading, fault-level assessment and dispatch constraints, which are common considerations in grid connection studies and power-system operation analysis.

技术深挖问答Technical Deep Dive FAQ

如何验证潮流计算结果？How were the load-flow results checked?

先将线路阻抗换算为标幺值并识别 Slack/PV/PQ 节点，再用线路潮流公式和节点功率平衡进行手算，最后与 PowerWorld 的线路潮流、母线电压和 Ybus 输出进行对比。该过程用于检查参数输入、方程设置和仿真输出是否一致。Line impedances were first converted into per-unit values and bus types were classified as Slack, PV or PQ. Manual line-flow and nodal-balance calculations were then compared with PowerWorld line-flow, bus-voltage and Ybus outputs to check consistency between input data, equations and simulation results.

无功负荷为什么会影响电压稳定？Why does reactive loading affect voltage stability?

无功需求增加会提高线路电流，并在线路和变压器的电抗上形成更大的电压降。Q-V 分析显示，随着无功负荷上升，远端母线电压从接近额定值下降，在高无功区间进入明显低电压状态。Higher reactive demand increases current and voltage drop across line and transformer reactance. The Q-V analysis showed that remote-bus voltage decreased as reactive loading increased, eventually reaching a low-voltage operating region under high reactive demand.

经济调度和最优潮流有什么区别？How do economic dispatch and optimal power flow differ?

经济调度关注发电成本最小化，若不考虑网络约束，可能使便宜机组集中出力并导致部分线路过载。最优潮流会把线路容量、电压和网络可行性纳入求解。在高负荷算例中，安全重调度将线路恢复到 100% MVA 限值以内，但小时成本从 26,465 英镑/小时增至 28,817 英镑/小时。Economic dispatch focuses on minimising generation cost and may overload lines if network constraints are ignored. Optimal power flow considers line capacity, voltage and network feasibility. In the high-loading case, secure redispatch brought all line loading within the 100% MVA limit, while hourly cost increased from 26,465 GBP/h to 28,817 GBP/h.

分布式电源接入会带来哪些电网影响？What grid impacts were considered for distributed generation?

报告讨论了分布式光伏和风电接入后可能出现的双向潮流、故障电流水平变化、保护配合复杂度提高，以及传统单向潮流保护策略可能不再充分适用的问题。The report discusses bidirectional power flow, changes in fault-current levels, increased protection-coordination complexity, and limitations of traditional protection strategies designed around one-directional power flow.
