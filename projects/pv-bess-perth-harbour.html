珀斯港光伏储能系统设计与技术经济分析 PV and Battery Energy Storage System Design for Perth Harbour

该项目来自爱丁堡大学太阳能及光伏系统（Solar Energy and Photovoltaic Systems）课程组项目。我负责澳大利亚珀斯（Perth）太阳能资源分析、住宅屋顶光伏（Photovoltaic, PV）方案，以及弗里曼特尔港（Fremantle Harbour）大型光伏储能系统（Photovoltaic and Battery Energy Storage System, PV-BESS）的工程设计、光伏仿真软件（PVsyst）建模、损失分析和经济评估。 This University of Edinburgh MSc group project compared grid-connected PV systems in Southampton and Perth. My core responsibility was the Fremantle Harbour PV-BESS case: translating port electricity demand, solar resource, available roof area and tariff assumptions into a simulated, costed engineering design.

项目概览（Situation, Task, Action, Result, STAR）Project Overview (STAR)

Situation
课程要求四人小组比较英国南安普敦（Southampton）与澳大利亚珀斯（Perth）的并网光伏系统，覆盖住宅屋顶和港口商业场景，并从太阳能资源、工程配置、经济性和减排效果进行评价。 The course required a four-person team to compare grid-connected PV systems in Southampton, UK and Perth, Australia, across residential rooftop and commercial port scenarios, considering solar resource, engineering design, economics and carbon savings.
Task
我负责珀斯太阳能资源分析和弗里曼特尔港大型光伏储能系统（Photovoltaic and Battery Energy Storage System, PV-BESS）方案，需要把港口用电目标、屋顶面积、气象数据和电价假设转化为可在光伏仿真软件（PVsyst）中验证的工程模型。 I was responsible for the Perth solar resource analysis and the Fremantle Harbour PV-BESS design, converting port load targets, roof area, weather data and tariff assumptions into an engineering model that could be validated in PVsyst.
Action
完成负荷目标推算、550 Wp 双面半片组件选型、26 台 30 kW 串型逆变器配置、电池储能系统（Battery Energy Storage System, BESS）容量计算、三维（3D）场景建模、近端遮挡和系统损失分析，并建立 20 年财务模型。 I estimated the design load target, selected 550 Wp bifacial half-cell modules, configured 26 x 30 kW string inverters, sized the BESS, built the 3D scene, analysed near-shading and system losses, and prepared a 20-year financial model.
Result
形成 939 kWp 光伏阵列、931.8 kWh 可用储能容量和 1.20 直流/交流容量比（Direct Current/Alternating Current ratio, DC/AC ratio）的方案；仿真年发电量 1263.2 MWh，性能比（Performance Ratio, PR）67.29%，平准化度电成本（Levelized Cost of Energy, LCOE）£0.1112/kWh，投资回收期 3.3 年，内部收益率（Internal Rate of Return, IRR）30.83%。 The final design used a 939 kWp PV array, 931.8 kWh usable storage and a 1.20 DC/AC ratio. Simulated annual production was 1263.2 MWh, with 67.29% PR, £0.1112/kWh LCOE, 3.3-year payback and 30.83% IRR.

项目边界Project Boundary

  • 地点：澳大利亚西澳州弗里曼特尔港客运站前停车场屋顶，报告坐标约为 32°02'54"S, 115°44'50"E。Location: car-park roof in front of the Fremantle passenger station, Western Australia, with report coordinates around 32°02'54"S, 115°44'50"E.
  • 目标：围绕港口商业负荷设计并网光伏与储能方案，而不是完整港口微电网控制系统。Goal: design a grid-connected PV and storage solution for a commercial harbour load, not a full port microgrid control system.
  • 仿真：使用光伏仿真软件（PVsyst）和气象数据库（Meteonorm 8.2）数据，重点评估发电量、损失链、性能比和财务可行性。Simulation: PVsyst with Meteonorm 8.2 weather data, focused on energy yield, loss chain, performance ratio and financial feasibility.
  • 项目输出：形成可复核的光伏储能系统方案，包含容量配置、三维建模、损失分析、成本模型和经济性评价。Project output: a reviewable PV-BESS design covering sizing, 3D modelling, loss analysis, cost modelling and economic evaluation.

容量配置与设计逻辑Sizing and Design Logic

需求推算Demand Estimate 根据报告使用的港口能耗数据，836 MWh/year 约对应 Victoria Quay 15% 用电；若覆盖 50%，目标电量约为 2786.67 MWh/year。Using the port energy data adopted in the report, 836 MWh/year corresponds to about 15% of Victoria Quay demand; a 50% target implies about 2786.67 MWh/year.
光伏阵列PV Array 1708 块 550 Wp 双面半片组件，总容量约 939 kWp，阵列面积约 4412 m²。1708 x 550 Wp bifacial half-cell modules, giving about 939 kWp and roughly 4412 m² array area.
逆变器Inverters 26 台 30 kWac 串型逆变器，总交流侧容量 780 kWac；直流/交流容量比约 1.20，用于提高逆变器利用率，并接受少量高辐照时段削峰风险。26 x 30 kWac string inverters, giving 780 kWac. The DC/AC ratio is about 1.20, increasing inverter utilisation while accepting limited clipping risk during high-irradiance periods.
储能Battery Storage 采用 BYD Battery Box Premium HVS 12.8 参数：512 V、25 Ah，91 个单元并联，理想容量 1164.8 kWh；按最低荷电状态（State of Charge, SOC）20% 计算，可用容量约 931.8 kWh。Based on BYD Battery Box Premium HVS 12.8 parameters: 512 V, 25 Ah and 91 units in parallel. Ideal capacity is 1164.8 kWh; with 20% minimum SOC, usable energy is about 931.8 kWh.
布局Layout 采用东西双拱（East-West Dome）低倾角行排，两个方位角约为 -39° 和 +141°，目的是提升早晚发电、降低行间遮挡并适配大面积平屋顶。An east-west dome low-tilt row layout was used, with azimuths around -39° and +141°, to improve morning/evening generation, reduce row-to-row shading and suit a large flat roof.

度电成本怎么算How LCOE Was Interpreted

平准化度电成本（Levelized Cost of Energy, LCOE）表示生命周期总成本与生命周期总发电量之比。本模型折现率设为 0%（课程基准假设），即未进行时间价值折现。Case 4 的 LCOE 为 £0.1112/kWh，来自光伏仿真软件经济模型输出，不应简单用总投资除以第一年发电量，因为模型还包含运营成本、折旧、替换准备金和长期发电衰减。LCOE is the ratio of lifetime total cost to lifetime total generation. This model uses a 0% discount rate (course baseline assumption), so no time-value discounting is applied. Case 4 LCOE is £0.1112/kWh from the PVsyst economic model; it should not be calculated simply as CAPEX divided by first-year output, because the model also includes OPEX, depreciation, replacement provisions and long-term generation degradation.

设计细节Design Details

关键工程参数说明Key Engineering Parameters

以下内容补充说明电池、成本、电价、度电成本和系统布局等关键参数，帮助读者理解方案的工程依据和经济假设。 The notes below explain key assumptions for the battery system, cost model, tariffs, LCOE and layout, helping readers understand the engineering basis and economic assumptions behind the design.

电池具体用了什么型号？容量是怎么来的？Which battery model was used, and how was capacity calculated?
报告采用 BYD Battery Box Premium HVS 12.8 的光伏仿真软件（PVsyst）数据库参数。单个电池单元按 512 V、25 Ah 计算，项目采用 1 串 91 并，因此理想容量为 1 × 91 × 512 V × 25 Ah = 1,164,800 Wh，即 1164.8 kWh。考虑 80% 放电深度（Depth of Discharge, DoD），可用容量约 931.8 kWh。报告还给出电池生命周期可储能量约 5534.7 MWh，用于说明该配置能够支持商业负荷削峰填谷，但仍不足以完全解决高光伏渗透率下的电网调节问题。 The report used PVsyst database parameters for BYD Battery Box Premium HVS 12.8. Each battery unit is modelled as 512 V and 25 Ah. With 1 in series and 91 in parallel, ideal capacity is 1 x 91 x 512 V x 25 Ah = 1,164,800 Wh, or 1164.8 kWh. At 80% depth of discharge, usable energy is about 931.8 kWh. The report also gives about 5534.7 MWh lifetime stored energy, supporting peak trimming and valley filling but not enough to fully regulate a high-PV-penetration port system.
为什么电池成本这么高？它在总投资里占多少？Why is the battery cost high, and how important is it in CAPEX?
成本页中电池为 91 个单元，单价 £3,911.98，总计 £355,990.11。相对于总安装成本 £678,036.83，电池约占 52.5%，是最大的单项成本。这也是为什么本项目虽然投资回收期短、内部收益率较高，但平准化度电成本（Levelized Cost of Energy, LCOE）仍高于英国港口 Case 3 的原因之一。 The cost page lists 91 battery units at £3,911.98 each, totalling £355,990.11. Against total installation cost of £678,036.83, batteries account for about 52.5%, the largest single item. This is one reason why the project has short payback and high IRR but a higher LCOE than the UK harbour Case 3.
度电成本是怎么计算的？为什么不能直接用投资除以年发电量？How was LCOE calculated, and why is it not just CAPEX divided by annual generation?
平准化度电成本（Levelized Cost of Energy, LCOE）的核心逻辑是生命周期总成本除以生命周期总发电量，并通常考虑折现、运营成本、维护、保险、税费、设备更换准备金和长期发电衰减。报告中 Case 4 的 LCOE 为 £0.1112/kWh，来自光伏仿真软件（PVsyst）经济模型输出。若只用 £678,036.83 除以第一年 1263.2 MWh，会忽略每年 £113,457.60 的运营成本、逆变器和电池更换准备金，因此会低估真实生命周期成本。 LCOE is lifetime cost divided by lifetime generation, usually with discounting, OPEX, maintenance, insurance, taxes, replacement provisions and long-term degradation. Case 4 LCOE is £0.1112/kWh from the PVsyst economic model. Dividing £678,036.83 by first-year generation of 1263.2 MWh would ignore annual OPEX of £113,457.60 and replacement provisions for inverters and batteries, so it would underestimate lifetime cost.
项目使用了什么电价？收益是按什么逻辑来的？Which tariffs were used, and how was revenue interpreted?
澳大利亚电价假设为峰时 £0.34/kWh、谷时 £0.06/kWh。英国对比案例采用 £0.20/kWh。对于港口光伏储能系统，收益不是只看“省下多少电费”，还要看自用电量、上网售电、储能移峰以及不同电价时段的价值。成本页给出有用太阳能电量约 414 MWh/year，上网售电量 850 MWh/year，说明项目有相当一部分收益来自富余电量上网。 The Australian tariff assumptions were £0.34/kWh at peak and £0.06/kWh off-peak; the UK comparison case used £0.20/kWh. For the harbour PV-BESS system, revenue is not only avoided electricity purchase; it also depends on self-use, grid export, battery shifting and tariff timing. The cost page lists useful solar energy of about 414 MWh/year and energy sold to the grid of 850 MWh/year, so a large part of the value comes from exporting surplus energy.
为什么直流/交流容量比选 1.20？Why was the DC/AC ratio set to 1.20?
光伏阵列直流侧容量约 939 kWp，逆变器交流侧容量为 26 × 30 kWac = 780 kWac，因此直流/交流容量比约 939/780 = 1.20。这样做的目的是在大多数非峰值辐照时段提高逆变器利用率，让逆变器更长时间运行在较高输出区间。代价是极少数强辐照时段可能出现限功率削峰，但项目认为该损失可接受，且有利于年发电量和经济性。 The DC array is about 939 kWp and AC inverter capacity is 26 x 30 kWac = 780 kWac, so DC/AC is about 939/780 = 1.20. The aim is to improve inverter utilisation during most non-peak irradiance periods and keep the inverter operating at higher output for longer. The trade-off is possible clipping during rare high-irradiance periods, considered acceptable for annual yield and economics.
为什么采用东西双拱布局，而不是单一朝向？Why use an east-west dome layout instead of one direction?
港口停车场屋顶面积大且接近平屋顶，适合低倾角东西双拱（East-West Dome）行排。两个朝向约为 -39° 和 +141°，可让早晨和下午都有较稳定输出，减少单一朝向在正午形成尖峰的问题，同时提高屋顶覆盖率。报告中的近端遮挡分析显示，大部分日间遮挡损失处于 1%-10% 范围，较高遮挡主要集中在 06:00-08:00 和 16:00-18:00 的低太阳高度角时段。 The large, nearly flat car-park roof suits a low-tilt east-west dome layout. The two azimuths, around -39° and +141°, provide steadier morning and afternoon output, reduce a single midday peak and improve roof coverage. The report's near-shading analysis shows most daytime shading loss within 1%-10%, with higher shading concentrated around 06:00-08:00 and 16:00-18:00.
