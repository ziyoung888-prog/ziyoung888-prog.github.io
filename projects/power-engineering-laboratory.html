电力工程实验：Buck 变换器测试与感应电机特性分析 Power Engineering Laboratory: Buck Converter Testing and Induction Motor Analysis

该实验项目把电力电子硬件、电机等效电路、实验测量和仿真分析放在同一个工程场景中训练。项目覆盖 Buck 变换器的示波器测量、功率分析仪读数、PCB 原理图和器件数据表理解、LTSpice 仿真，以及三相鼠笼式感应电机的空载、堵转、转矩-转速和启动暂态分析。 This laboratory project connected power-electronics hardware, machine equivalent circuits, experimental measurement and simulation analysis. It covered buck-converter oscilloscope measurement, power-analyser readings, PCB schematic and datasheet interpretation, LTSpice simulation, and squirrel-cage induction motor testing.

为什么学习这个项目Why I Studied This Project

电气工程岗位不只要求会做软件仿真，还要求理解真实设备的测量误差、保护动作、控制响应和安全边界。Buck 变换器对应电源、电驱、储能和新能源设备中的功率变换环节；感应电机对应工业驱动、轨道交通、泵、风机和制造设备中的常见负载。学习这个实验的意义在于把课堂模型落到真实硬件：同一个结论需要能被仪器测量、仿真模型和工程解释共同支撑。 Electrical engineering roles require more than software simulation; they also require understanding measurement error, protection behaviour, control response and safety limits in real equipment. Buck converters relate to power supplies, drives, storage systems and renewable-energy hardware, while induction motors are common industrial loads in manufacturing, rail, pumps and fans.

项目定位Project Positioning

这是一个硕士课程实验项目，适合作为电力电子测试、电机分析、设备调试、测试工程和技术支持岗位的证据型项目。它的价值不在于商业落地，而在于展示对真实电气实验流程的理解：读懂原理图，安全连接设备，采集数据，用模型复算结果，再解释实验和理论之间的差异。 This is an MSc laboratory project suitable for demonstrating practical readiness for power-electronics testing, machine analysis, equipment commissioning, test engineering and technical-support roles. Its value lies in connecting schematics, safe setup, measurements, model-based calculation and engineering interpretation.

STAR 项目逻辑STAR Project Logic

情境Situation

课程要求在电力实验室中完成 Buck 变换器和鼠笼式感应电机实验，设备涉及电源、负载、示波器、功率分析仪、PCB 和旋转电机，需要严格遵守电气安全规范。The laboratory required work on a buck converter and squirrel-cage induction motor using power supplies, loads, oscilloscopes, power analysers, PCB schematics and rotating-machine equipment under strict electrical safety practice.

任务Task

围绕效率、纹波、负载调整率、限流保护、轻载模式、反馈增益、电机等效电路参数、转矩-转速曲线和启动暂态，形成可用于口试答辩的结果展示。The task was to produce viva-ready results covering efficiency, ripple, load regulation, current limiting, light-load modes, feedback gain, motor equivalent-circuit parameters, torque-speed curves and start-up transients.

行动Action

使用示波器和功率分析仪采集 Buck 变换器输入、输出和波形数据；用 LTSpice 对开关频率、栅极电阻、同步 Buck 模式和轻载工作方式进行仿真；基于空载和堵转数据估算电机等效电路参数，并用 MATLAB 计算转矩、电流、输出功率和效率曲线。I used oscilloscope and power-analyser measurements for converter data, LTSpice for switching-frequency, gate-resistance, synchronous-mode and light-load simulations, and MATLAB with no-load and locked-rotor data to calculate machine torque, current, output power and efficiency curves.

结果Result

输出了 Buck 变换器效率和保护行为分析、电机等效电路参数、实验曲线与理论曲线对比，以及电网供电和发电机供电启动时间差异解释。项目把“测量-建模-仿真-解释”的工程闭环练习完整串联起来。The project produced converter efficiency and protection analysis, machine equivalent-circuit parameters, measured-versus-theoretical curve comparison, and an explanation of grid-fed versus generator-fed start-up time differences.

技术流程Technical Workflow

阶段 1：安全与硬件熟悉Stage 1: Safety and Hardware Familiarisation

阅读实验手册，确认电源、电机、负载、测量端口和跳线设置；在改变 Buck 板跳线前关闭电源，按实验要求完成安全连接。Reviewed the manuals, identified supplies, machines, load, measurement points and jumper settings, and followed safe setup procedures before changing board configuration.

阶段 2：Buck 变换器测量与仿真Stage 2: Buck Measurement and Simulation

在 10 V 输入、3.3 V 输出设定下测试不同负载电流，比较实验效率和 LTSpice 仿真结果，并分析开关频率、输入输出电压比、栅极电阻和同步 Buck 模式对效率与开关损耗的影响。Measured load-current conditions at 10 V input and 3.3 V output, compared laboratory efficiency with LTSpice simulation, and analysed switching frequency, voltage ratio, gate resistance and SyncBuck mode.

阶段 3：保护、反馈与动态响应Stage 3: Protection, Feedback and Dynamics

分析负载调整率、ILIM 限流、短路瞬态、轻载模式、反馈增益、电容大小和负载类型对输出电压、纹波、过冲和恢复时间的影响。Analysed load regulation, ILIM current limiting, short-circuit transient behaviour, light-load modes, feedback gain, capacitor size and load type effects on ripple, overshoot and recovery time.

阶段 4：感应电机建模与启动暂态Stage 4: Motor Modelling and Start-up Transients

根据空载和堵转测试估算单相等效电路参数，绘制转矩、定子电流、输出功率和效率随转速变化的曲线，并比较电网供电、发电机供电和理论模型启动时间。Estimated per-phase equivalent-circuit parameters from no-load and locked-rotor tests, plotted torque, stator current, output power and efficiency versus speed, and compared grid-fed, generator-fed and theoretical start-up times.

关键输出Selected Outputs

  • Buck 变换器同步模式对比：同步 Buck 开启时效率为 82.84%，关闭时为 78.31%，同一输出功率下同步开关改善了效率。SyncBuck comparison: efficiency was 82.84% with SyncBuck on and 78.31% with SyncBuck off at the recorded operating point.
  • 电容纹波对比：23.5 uF 下测量纹波 3.41 mV、理论值 3.299 mV；9.4 uF 下测量纹波 8.7 mV、理论值 8.247 mV。Capacitor-ripple comparison: 23.5 uF produced 3.41 mV measured ripple versus 3.299 mV theoretical; 9.4 uF produced 8.7 mV versus 8.247 mV.
  • 电机等效电路参数：R1=16.5，R2'=16.6473，X1=X2'=16.5318，Rc=1152，Xm=288.4689。Motor equivalent-circuit parameters: R1=16.5, R2'=16.6473, X1=X2'=16.5318, Rc=1152 and Xm=288.4689.
  • 电机实验结果：最大输出功率 27.8973 W，出现在 1075 rpm；最高效率 40.1691%，出现在 1207 rpm。Motor laboratory results: maximum output power was 27.8973 W at 1075 rpm; maximum efficiency was 40.1691% at 1207 rpm.
  • 启动暂态对比：电网供电启动时间 1.05 s，孤立发电机供电启动时间 2.65 s，理论负载模型结果 0.446 s，无耦合负载理论结果 0.3558 s。Start-up comparison: grid-fed start-up time was 1.05 s, isolated generator-fed start-up time was 2.65 s, load-model theory was 0.446 s and no-coupled-load theory was 0.3558 s.

对个人能力与岗位需求的联系Relevance to Engineering Roles

这个项目强化了从真实设备出发做工程判断的能力：能读懂 PCB 原理图和数据表，能使用示波器与功率分析仪采集关键变量，能把 LTSpice 和 MATLAB 结果用于解释实验现象，也能在模型与实测不一致时说明摩擦、风阻、铜耗、温度、谐波、漏磁和器件非理想因素的影响。它与电力电子测试工程师、电机测试工程师、新能源设备调试、储能/逆变器技术支持、设备运维和工程验证岗位相关。 This project strengthened practical engineering judgement: reading PCB schematics and datasheets, measuring key variables with oscilloscope and power analyser, using LTSpice and MATLAB to interpret results, and explaining why measured behaviour differs from ideal models because of friction, windage, copper loss, temperature, harmonics, leakage flux and non-ideal device behaviour.

项目问答Project FAQ

这个实验最核心的学习价值是什么？What was the main learning value?

核心价值是把电力电子和电机课程中的理论模型放到真实实验设备中检验。Buck 变换器部分训练了对开关电源效率、纹波、反馈和保护动作的理解；电机部分训练了从实验数据估算等效电路参数，并用 MATLAB 对转矩、效率和启动暂态进行解释。The main value was testing theoretical power-electronics and machine models against real laboratory equipment, covering converter efficiency, ripple, feedback and protection behaviour, and motor equivalent-circuit estimation with MATLAB-based interpretation.

为什么这个项目和岗位相关？Why is this relevant to engineering roles?

很多电气工程岗位需要处理“设备实际表现”和“模型预期结果”之间的差异。这个实验包含硬件连接、仪器测量、仿真复核、保护逻辑分析和结果汇报，能够对应测试工程、设备调试、电力电子应用、电机驱动和技术支持中的基础工作方式。Many electrical roles involve the gap between real equipment behaviour and model expectations. This project involved hardware setup, measurement, simulation checking, protection analysis and result presentation, which map to testing, commissioning, power-electronics application, motor-drive and technical-support work.

实验和理论结果为什么会不同？Why did measured and theoretical results differ?

电机模型没有完全包含摩擦、风阻、转子和定子铜耗、温度变化、谐波损耗、漏磁以及实际转换比例等因素。Buck 变换器也会受到 MOSFET 开关损耗、栅极驱动、PCB 寄生参数、测量探头和负载状态影响，因此实验结果需要结合非理想因素解释。The motor model did not fully include friction, windage, copper loss, temperature variation, harmonic loss, leakage flux and actual conversion-ratio uncertainty. The converter was also affected by MOSFET switching loss, gate drive, PCB parasitics, probe effects and load condition.
