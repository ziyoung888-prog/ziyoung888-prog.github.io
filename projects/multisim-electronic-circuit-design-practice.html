电子电路综合设计实践：模拟电路与数字电路仿真Electronic Circuit Design Practice: Analogue and Digital Simulation

该项目基于电基础设计实践材料，使用 NI Multisim 完成模拟电路和数字电路两个模块的设计与仿真。模拟部分围绕 OCL 音频功率放大器展开，覆盖输出功率、负载阻抗、频率响应、偏置和失真检查；数字部分覆盖交通信号灯控制器、数字钟、频率计、抢答器等逻辑系统，强调从指标分解、参数计算到仿真验证和报告输出的完整流程。This academic design-practice project uses NI Multisim for both analogue and digital circuit work. The analogue part focuses on an OCL audio power amplifier, while the digital part covers a traffic signal controller, digital clock, frequency meter and responder logic, connecting specification breakdown, calculation, simulation and reporting.

项目叙事Project Story

项目背景Context

电子电路设计需要把电气指标转化为电路拓扑、元件参数和可验证的仿真波形，同时兼顾模拟电路与数字逻辑两类系统。Electronic circuit design converts electrical specifications into topology, component values and verifiable simulation waveforms across analogue and digital systems.

工程目标Objective

完成 OCL 音频功率放大器和多类数字逻辑系统设计，覆盖频响、功率、计数、译码、锁存和状态切换。The objective was to design an OCL audio amplifier and digital logic systems covering frequency response, power, counting, decoding, latching and state switching.

技术执行Action

使用 NI Multisim 完成参数计算、原理图搭建、AC 扫描、瞬态分析、逻辑器件连接和七段显示仿真。NI Multisim was used for parameter calculation, schematic modelling, AC sweep, transient analysis, logic-device wiring and seven-segment display simulation.

关键结果Result

形成覆盖 OCL 放大器、数字钟、频率计、抢答器和交通灯控制器的综合仿真成果，交通灯逻辑包含 30/20/5 秒时序。The project produced simulations for an OCL amplifier, digital clock, frequency meter, responder and traffic-light controller, including 30/20/5s traffic timing.

项目动机与岗位价值Project Motivation and Role Value

为什么要做Why this project

这是本科电子电路综合设计项目。本科阶段学习模拟电子和数字电子后，我需要把课本中的放大器、计数器、译码器、触发器和 555 定时器转化为能被仿真验证的系统。Multisim 提供了一个从计算到原理图再到波形检查的练习环境。This was an undergraduate electronic-circuit design project. After studying analogue and digital electronics, I needed to turn textbook amplifiers, counters, decoders, flip-flops and 555 timers into verifiable simulated systems. Multisim provided a workflow from calculation to schematic and waveform checking.

工程意义Engineering meaning

项目的意义在于让我理解电子电路不是单个公式，而是指标、拓扑、元件参数和实际波形之间的匹配。OCL 功率放大器训练频响、输出功率和失真判断；数字系统训练计数、译码、锁存和状态切换。The project showed that circuit design is not a single formula; it is matching specifications, topology, component values and observed waveforms. The OCL amplifier trained frequency response, output power and distortion judgement; the digital systems trained counting, decoding, latching and state switching.

能力与岗位连接Ability and role connection

这个项目对应硬件测试、电子助理工程师、设备调试、质量验证和技术支持岗位。它提升了我从指标推导参数、搭建仿真电路、阅读波形、定位异常并写出技术报告的基础能力。The project maps to hardware testing, junior electronics engineering, equipment debugging, quality verification and technical support. It improved specification-to-parameter reasoning, circuit simulation, waveform reading, issue localisation and technical reporting.

问题背景Problem Context

电路设计实践需要把抽象指标转化为具体电路拓扑、元件参数和可验证的仿真结果。模拟电路侧重增益、功率、频率响应和失真；数字电路侧重计数、译码、状态切换和时序控制。Multisim 为该项目提供了从原理图搭建到波形、频响和逻辑状态验证的统一环境。Circuit design practice converts abstract specifications into circuit topology, component values and verifiable simulation results. Analogue circuits focus on gain, power, frequency response and distortion; digital circuits focus on counting, decoding, state switching and timing control.

技术流程Technical Workflow

阶段一Stage 1根据输出功率、负载阻抗、频率响应和输入阻抗指标，选择 OCL/OTL 互补对称功率放大拓扑，并计算最大输出电压、电源电压和偏置参数。Select OCL/OTL complementary power amplifier topology from output-power, load, frequency-response and input-impedance targets, then calculate output voltage, supply voltage and bias parameters.
阶段二Stage 2在 Multisim 中搭建音频功率放大器，运行 AC 扫描、瞬态分析和虚拟仪器测量，检查通频带、输出波形、失真和静态工作点。Build the audio power amplifier in Multisim and run AC sweep, transient analysis and virtual-instrument measurements for bandwidth, output waveform, distortion and operating point checks.
阶段三Stage 3设计数字逻辑系统，包括数字钟、频率计、抢答器和交通信号灯控制器，使用计数器、译码器、触发器、锁存器和组合逻辑实现功能。Design digital logic systems including a digital clock, frequency meter, responder and traffic signal controller using counters, decoders, flip-flops, latches and combinational logic.
阶段四Stage 4对交通信号灯控制器进行时序设计：主干道 30 秒、支干道 20 秒、黄灯 5 秒，使用 LED 和七段显示器验证状态切换与倒计时显示。Develop traffic signal timing with 30s main-road green, 20s branch-road green and 5s yellow intervals, then verify state switching and countdown display with LEDs and seven-segment displays.

  • 完成 OCL 音频功率放大器的拓扑选择、工作点设计、元器件参数计算和 Multisim 仿真验证。Completed OCL audio amplifier topology selection, operating-point design, component calculation and Multisim verification.
  • 使用 AC 扫描和瞬态分析检查 20Hz-20kHz 频率响应、输出波形和失真情况。Used AC sweep and transient analysis to check 20Hz-20kHz response, output waveform and distortion behaviour.
