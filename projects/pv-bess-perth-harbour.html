Perth Harbour 光伏及储能系统设计 Perth Harbour PV and Battery Energy Storage System Design

该项目来自 Solar Energy Group Project，面向港口商业用电场景，完成并网光伏与储能系统的技术经济设计。页面展示工程问题、设计方法、仿真结果和项目反思。 This project comes from the Solar Energy Group Project and focuses on a commercial harbour electricity scenario. It presents the techno-economic design of a grid-connected PV and battery storage system, covering the engineering problem, design method, simulation results and project reflection.

项目概览（STAR） Project Overview (STAR)

Situation
课程项目要求比较英国 Southampton 与澳大利亚 Perth 的并网光伏系统设计，覆盖住宅屋顶和港口大型场景，并从发电能力、经济收益、CO2 减排和政策环境进行综合评价。 The group project compared grid-connected PV system designs in Southampton, UK and Perth, Australia, covering residential rooftop and harbour-scale cases, with evaluation across generation, economics, CO2 savings and policy context.
Task
我负责 Australia solar resource 分析和 Perth Harbour 大型光伏系统设计，需要基于港口用电目标、日照资源、安装空间和并网经济性，提出可仿真的 PV-BESS 方案。 I was responsible for the Australia solar resource analysis and the Perth Harbour large-scale PV system design, translating port energy demand, solar resource, installation area and grid economics into a PV-BESS design that could be simulated.
Action
我基于 Fremantle Ports 年报中的能耗信息推算设计目标，选择 550 Wp 双面半片组件、30 kW 逆变器和 BYD 512 V 电池模型，在 PVsyst 中建立 3D 模型，采用 east-west dome 布局，分析近端遮挡、归一化发电量、PR、经济性和储能支撑作用。 I estimated the design target from Fremantle Ports energy data, selected 550 Wp bifacial half-cell modules, 30 kW inverters and a BYD 512 V battery model, built the PVsyst 3D model, applied an east-west dome layout, and analysed near-shading, normalised production, PR, economics and the role of storage.
Result
方案形成 939 kWp 阵列、1164.8 kWh BESS、1.20 DC/AC 比，年发电约 1263.2 MWh，IRR 30.83%，回收期 3.3 年，30 年累计减排约 27,257.7 吨 CO2。项目也明确指出当前储能容量仍不足以完全解决高渗透率 PV 的调节问题，后续应扩大储能和优化调度。 The design produced a 939 kWp PV array, 1164.8 kWh BESS and 1.20 DC/AC ratio, with annual production of about 1263.2 MWh, IRR of 30.83%, payback of 3.3 years and around 27,257.7 tons of CO2 savings over 30 years. The project also identified that the current storage capacity was not enough for full regulation under high PV penetration, so future work should expand storage and optimise dispatch.

设计逻辑 Design Logic

  • 需求推算：Fremantle Ports 年报提到 836 MWh/year 可满足 Victoria Quay 约 15% 用电，若目标为 50%，对应 2786.67 MWh/year。Demand estimate: Fremantle Ports reported that 836 MWh/year could meet around 15% of Victoria Quay energy needs; a 50% target corresponds to 2786.67 MWh/year.
  • 最小阵列功率：按澳大利亚 8-9 h/day 平均日照估算，最小阵列功率约 848.3 kW。Minimum array power: using 8-9 h/day average sunshine in Australia, the estimated minimum array power was about 848.3 kW.
  • 组件配置：选择 Mono 550 Wp 双面半片组件，总装机约 939 kWp，对应 939 kW / 550 W ≈ 1708 块组件。Module configuration: selected Mono 550 Wp bifacial half-cell modules; 939 kWp total capacity corresponds to about 1708 modules.
  • 面积计算：单块组件约 1.134 m × 2.278 m，总阵列面积约 4412.19 m2。Area calculation: each module is about 1.134 m x 2.278 m, giving an array area of about 4412.19 m2.
  • 逆变器配置：26 台 30 kW AC 组串逆变器，总 AC 容量 780 kW，DC/AC 比约 1.20，用于提高强日照时段组件潜力利用。Inverter configuration: 26 string inverters rated at 30 kW AC, giving 780 kW AC capacity and a DC/AC ratio of about 1.20 to use module potential during high-irradiance hours.
  • 储能配置：BYD 512 V HVS12.8，1 串 91 并，理想容量 1 x 91 x 512 V x 25 Ah = 1164.8 kWh；80% DoD 下可用容量约 932 kWh。Storage configuration: BYD 512 V HVS12.8, 1 series and 91 parallel, ideal capacity 1 x 91 x 512 V x 25 Ah = 1164.8 kWh; usable capacity is about 932 kWh at 80% DoD.

PVsyst 建模与损失分析 PVsyst Modelling and Loss Analysis

  • 位置：Perth Harbour 项目位于 passenger station 前大型停车场屋顶，纬度 32°02'54"S，经度 115°44'50"E。Location: the Perth Harbour system was placed on the large car-park roof in front of the passenger station, at latitude 32°02'54"S and longitude 115°44'50"E.
  • 布局：采用 Rows as domes east-west 布局，两个朝向约为 -39° 和 +141°，形成东西双面结构，提升早晚时段捕光并减少行间遮挡。Layout: used a rows-as-domes east-west layout with azimuths around -39° and +141°, forming a two-sided structure to capture morning and afternoon sunlight and reduce row-to-row shading.
  • 遮挡：近端遮挡损失大多控制在 1%-10%，主要集中在 6:00-8:00 和 16:00-18:00；20%-40% 的遮挡峰值持续时间有限。Shading: near-shading loss was mostly controlled within 1%-10%, concentrated around 6:00-8:00 and 16:00-18:00; 20%-40% peak shading periods were limited in duration.
  • 归一化发电：有用输出约 3.83 kWh/kWp/day，collection loss 约 1.25 kWh/kWp/day，system operation loss 约 0.39 kWh/kWp/day。Normalised production: useful output was about 3.83 kWh/kWp/day, collection loss about 1.25 kWh/kWp/day, and system operation loss about 0.39 kWh/kWp/day.
  • 性能比：月度 PR 在 0.65-0.70 间波动，年平均 PR 约 0.673；12 月和 1 月发电最高，6 月和 7 月较低。Performance ratio: monthly PR fluctuated around 0.65-0.70, with annual average PR about 0.673; production was highest in December and January and lower in June and July.

经济与环境结果 Economic and Environmental Results

  • 总安装投资：£678,036.83；年运营成本：£113,457.60。Total installation investment: £678,036.83; annual operating cost: £113,457.60.
  • 年系统发电：1263.2 MWh；售电上网：850 MWh/year；峰时电价假设：£0.34/kWh。Annual production: 1263.2 MWh; energy sold to grid: 850 MWh/year; peak tariff assumption: £0.34/kWh.
  • 经济性：回收期 3.3 年，NPV £3,980,509，IRR 30.83%，ROI 587.1%，LCOE £0.1112/kWh。Economics: payback 3.3 years, NPV £3,980,509, IRR 30.83%, ROI 587.1%, LCOE £0.1112/kWh.
  • 环境效益：30 年累计减排约 27,257.7 吨 CO2。Environmental benefit: around 27,257.7 tons of CO2 savings over 30 years.
  • 对比结论：与 Southampton Harbour Case 3 相比，Case 4 的回收期更短、IRR 和 ROI 更高，更适合强调短期回报和减排目标的商业港口场景。Comparison: compared with Southampton Harbour Case 3, Case 4 had shorter payback and higher IRR and ROI, making it more suitable for commercial port scenarios prioritising short-term return and emissions reduction.
