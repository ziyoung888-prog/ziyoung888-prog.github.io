电力系统潮流、故障、电压控制与安全经济调度分析 Load Flow, Fault, Voltage Control and Secure Economic Dispatch Analysis

该项目整合了四份课程研究报告，覆盖电力系统稳态潮流、导纳矩阵建模、短路故障水平、时序负荷、电压稳定、变压器有载调压、经济调度和分布式电源并网影响。项目价值在于把课堂公式转化为 PowerWorld 与 MATLAB 中可复核的工程分析流程。 This portfolio consolidates four coursework reports into one engineering case study covering steady-state load flow, admittance-matrix modelling, short-circuit fault level, time-varying load, voltage stability, OLTC voltage control, economic dispatch and distributed-generation impacts. Its value is the translation of textbook equations into auditable PowerWorld and MATLAB analysis workflows.

项目概览（STAR）Project Overview (STAR)

Situation
课程要求在多个给定电网算例中分析潮流、线路约束、故障水平、电压稳定和运行经济性，任务接近电网规划与运行工程师日常面对的仿真分析问题。 The course required analysis of load flow, line constraints, fault levels, voltage stability and operating economics across several network cases, matching practical simulation tasks in grid planning and operations.
Task
我需要把线路阻抗、负荷、发电机、变压器和控制约束转化为可计算模型，并解释每一次仿真变化背后的电气机理，而不是只报告软件截图。 I needed to convert line impedance, load, generators, transformers and operating constraints into computable models, then explain the electrical mechanism behind each simulation result rather than only present screenshots.
Action
使用手算、MATLAB 和 PowerWorld 交叉验证母线电压、相角、线路潮流、无功支撑、故障电流、过载路径和调度成本，并在高负荷、故障、无功需求上升和发电重分配场景中判断系统风险。 I cross-checked bus voltage, angle, line flow, reactive support, fault current, overload paths and dispatch cost using manual calculations, MATLAB and PowerWorld, then assessed system risk under high-load, fault, reactive-demand and redispatch scenarios.
Result
最终形成了一套可展示的电力系统分析证据链：从单个三母线系统的 Kirchhoff 平衡验证，到较大网络的时序电压、OLTC 调压、经济调度与安全约束冲突分析。 The final output is a presentable evidence chain for power-system analysis: from Kirchhoff balance verification in a three-bus case to time-series voltage, OLTC control, economic dispatch and security-constraint analysis in larger networks.

我做了什么What I Actually Did

  • 建立三母线和多母线网络模型，识别 Slack、PV、PQ 节点，并把线路 R/X 参数换算为标幺值。Built three-bus and multi-bus network models, identified Slack, PV and PQ buses, and converted line R/X data into per-unit values.
  • 用 MATLAB 求解导纳矩阵、节点电压、线路电流和复功率，并与 PowerWorld 输出进行一致性检查。Used MATLAB to solve admittance matrices, bus voltages, line currents and complex power flows, then checked consistency against PowerWorld output.
  • 对短路故障、负荷切除、发电机断开和高无功负荷场景进行分析，解释过载、电压跌落和电压崩溃风险。Analysed short-circuit faults, load disconnection, generator outage and high-reactive-load cases, explaining overload, voltage sag and voltage-collapse risk.
  • 在经济调度结果导致线路超载时，手动重分配发电出力，使线路负载回到 100% MVA 限值以内，并说明成本与安全性的取舍。When economic dispatch created overloads, I manually redistributed generator outputs to bring line loading back within the 100% MVA limit and explained the cost-security trade-off.

四份报告如何构成一个完整项目How the Four Reports Form One Project

Assignment 1 从三母线系统入手，计算线路标幺阻抗、节点类型、功率平衡和线路潮流；用 PowerWorld 对比手算结果，验证 Slack bus 对损耗和平衡的作用。Started with a three-bus system: per-unit line impedance, bus type classification, power balance and line-flow calculation; compared manual results with PowerWorld and verified the role of the slack bus in loss balancing.
Assignment 2 把网络表示为导纳矩阵和节点方程，用 MATLAB 求解电压、电流和功率流；随后在 PowerWorld 中做故障水平、负荷切除、发电机断开和电压稳定分析。Represented the network using admittance matrices and nodal equations, solved voltages, currents and power flows in MATLAB, then used PowerWorld for fault-level, load-disconnection, generator-outage and voltage-stability studies.
Assignment 3 处理 24 小时时序负荷，把标幺负荷转换为 MW/MVAr，比较平均与最大负荷工况，分析低压母线、T3 变压器和 L67 线路的运行压力，并用 OLTC 改善电压。Handled 24-hour time-varying loads, converted per-unit load into MW/MVAr, compared average and maximum loading, analysed stress on low-voltage buses, transformer T3 and line L67, and used OLTC action to improve voltage.
Assignment 4 使用 MATLAB 拉格朗日法求经济调度，再在 PowerWorld 中研究高负荷网络约束。通过降低北部发电、提高南部发电，把多条超载线路恢复到安全范围，并讨论 DG 并网对故障水平和保护的影响。Used a MATLAB Lagrange method for economic dispatch, then studied network constraints under high load in PowerWorld. By reducing northern generation and increasing southern generation, overloaded lines were restored to safe limits, followed by a discussion of DG impacts on fault level and protection.

方法与工具Methods and Tools

  • PowerWorld：搭建网络拓扑、输入发电机/负荷/线路/变压器参数，执行潮流、故障、差分模式和调度场景分析。PowerWorld: built network topology, entered generator/load/line/transformer data, and ran load-flow, fault, difference-mode and dispatch scenario studies.
  • MATLAB：构建 Ybus 矩阵、求解节点方程、计算线路电流和复功率，并实现拉格朗日经济调度计算。MATLAB: built Ybus matrices, solved nodal equations, calculated line current and complex power, and implemented Lagrange economic dispatch calculations.
  • 工程判断：把电压跌落、线路过载、无功不足和网络拥塞解释为物理问题，而不是只依赖软件输出。Engineering judgement: interpreted voltage drop, line overload, reactive-power shortage and network congestion as physical problems, not just software outputs.

对应岗位能力Role-Relevant Capabilities

  • 电力系统仿真：能把题目参数整理成可运行模型，并检查模型是否满足功率平衡和运行约束。Power-system simulation: converting specifications into executable models and checking power balance and operating constraints.
  • 运行分析：能识别线路、变压器、母线电压和无功支撑的薄弱环节。Operational analysis: identifying weak points in lines, transformers, bus voltage and reactive support.
  • 技术沟通：能把公式、仿真截图和结论组织成英文工程报告。Technical communication: organising equations, simulation evidence and conclusions into English engineering reports.

关键结果与可复述亮点Key Results and Interview Talking Points

潮流校验Load-Flow Verification 在三母线算例中，将手算线路潮流与 PowerWorld 仿真结果进行对比，证明节点功率输入输出满足 Kirchhoff 平衡。In the three-bus case, manual line-flow calculations were compared with PowerWorld results, proving that bus-level power input and output satisfied Kirchhoff balance.
电压稳定Voltage Stability 随着无功负荷升高，远端母线电压从接近额定值逐步跌落，并在高无功区间表现出电压崩溃风险，说明本地无功补偿和电压控制的重要性。As reactive load increased, remote-bus voltage fell from near-rated levels toward a voltage-collapse risk region, highlighting the importance of local reactive compensation and voltage control.
时序负荷Time-Series Loading 在 Assignment 3 中识别出高负荷时段集中在上午到下午，T3 变压器和 33 kV 线路 L67 接近额定容量，低压端母线更容易出现电压偏低。Assignment 3 identified that high loading concentrated from morning to afternoon, transformer T3 and 33 kV line L67 approached rated capacity, and downstream low-voltage buses were more exposed to undervoltage.
安全调度Secure Dispatch 在 Assignment 4 中，经济调度导致 Bus 1-3、Bus 1-2、Bus 2-5 等线路严重超载；通过降低 G1/G2、提高 G6 输出，最终把所有线路负载降至 100% MVA 限值以内，小时成本从 26,465 £/h 增至 28,817 £/h，体现安全约束的成本。In Assignment 4, economic dispatch overloaded lines such as Bus 1-3, Bus 1-2 and Bus 2-5. By reducing G1/G2 and increasing G6 output, all line loading was brought within the 100% MVA limit, while hourly cost increased from 26,465 GBP/h to 28,817 GBP/h, demonstrating the cost of security constraints.
新能源并网DG Integration 报告讨论了分布式光伏、风电等 DG 接入后对双向潮流、故障电流、保护配合和传统继电保护策略的影响。The reports discussed how distributed PV, wind and other DG sources affect bidirectional power flow, fault current, protection coordination and conventional relay strategies.

项目价值Project Value

  • 它证明我能从原始电网参数出发，独立建立、求解、校验和解释电力系统模型。It proves that I can start from raw grid parameters and independently build, solve, verify and explain power-system models.
  • 它把“经济最优”和“物理可行”区分开来，体现我理解电力系统运行中成本、安全和可靠性之间的真实取舍。It distinguishes economic optimum from physical feasibility, showing understanding of the real trade-off among cost, security and reliability in power-system operation.
  • 它与新能源岗位直接相关，因为新能源并网、储能调度和配电网规划都需要潮流、故障、电压和保护意识。It is directly relevant to renewable-energy roles because renewable integration, storage dispatch and distribution planning all require load-flow, fault, voltage and protection awareness.

复盘与局限Review and Limitations

  • 课程算例规模有限，重点在工程机理和方法训练，不代表真实电网全量数据建模。The coursework cases are limited in scale and focus on engineering mechanisms and methods, not full-scale utility network modelling.
  • 部分结论依赖给定参数和仿真假设，真实工程还需要设备铭牌、保护定值、实际负荷曲线和并网规范。Some conclusions depend on supplied parameters and simulation assumptions; real projects would require equipment ratings, protection settings, measured load profiles and grid-code requirements.
  • 后续可扩展为 N-1 安全校验、无功补偿优化、DG 容量接入上限评估和调度灵敏度分析。Future extension could add N-1 security checks, reactive-compensation optimisation, DG hosting-capacity assessment and dispatch sensitivity analysis.
